The Domain Challenge Real-World Transport Telemetry Realities

Tickets Sold ≠ Passengers on Board

Raw electronic ticketing machine (ETM) telemetry presents five major transport challenges:

1. Accumulation vs Point Events
A ticket only records boarding and alighting. Physical passenger count requires grouping by trip run: L_s = L_{s-1} + b_s - a_s with terminal clearance L_end = 0.
2. Free Pink Pass Holders
Female commuters travel free on Pink Passes without buying point-to-point tickets. We apply a calibrated inflation factor γ_r ∈ [1.20, 1.45].
3. Bus Capacity Normalization
60 passengers on a 12m standard bus (cap 70) means standing room; on a 9m midi bus (cap 45), it is severe overcrowding. We map load into 0–100 RCI.
4. Sparse Off-Peak Observation
Off-peak or feeder routes have low sample density. CrowdWise outputs 3-level confidence (High/Medium/Low) based on Random Forest tree spread.
